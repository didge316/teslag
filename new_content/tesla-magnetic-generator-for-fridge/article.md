# Tesla Magnetic Generator for Fridge: Keep Food Cold Off-Grid (2026)

Running a refrigerator is one of the biggest challenges for off-grid power — it runs 24/7 and has high starting wattage. A Tesla magnetic generator is one of the best solutions for keeping your fridge running off-grid.

Here's why and how.

## Refrigerator Power Requirements

| Fridge Type | Running Watts | Starting Watts | Daily kWh |
|------------|--------------|----------------|-----------|
| Small (5 cu ft) | 100–150W | 300–500W | 1.5–2.0 |
| Medium (10 cu ft) | 150–250W | 500–800W | 2.0–3.0 |
| Large (15+ cu ft) | 250–400W | 800–1,200W | 3.0–5.0 |
| Energy Star | 50–100W | 200–400W | 0.8–1.5 |

## Best Generator Setup for Fridge

### Minimum Setup

- **Generator:** DIY bifilar pancake coil (200–500W output)
- **Prime mover:** Wind turbine or bicycle drive
- **Battery:** 100–200Ah LiFePO4 @ 24V or 48V
- **Inverter:** 1,000W pure sine wave
- **Fridge:** Energy Star model (50–100W running)

### Key: Use an Energy Star Fridge

An Energy Star refrigerator uses 50–100W running vs. 250–400W for older models. This cuts your generator size and battery bank in half.

## Fridge-Specific Tips

1. **Choose DC fridge if possible** — 12V/24V/48V DC fridges eliminate inverter loss
2. **Use a DC compressor fridge** — Dometic, IceCo, or Engel DC models
3. **Keep it full** — Full fridges hold temperature longer during low generation
4. **Place in cool area** — Cooler ambient = less compressor runtime
5. **Check door seals** — Leaky seals force compressor to run more

## Conclusion

A magnetic generator is ideal for off-grid refrigerator power. Pair it with an Energy Star or DC compressor fridge and a modest battery bank for reliable, continuous cooling.
