## Maximizing Your Monthly Savings

### 1. Optimize Your Prime Mover
The generator only produces power when driven. Maximize drive efficiency:
- **Wind**: Position in open areas, away from buildings and trees
- **Water**: Use flowing streams or create a small channel
- **Manual**: Use a bicycle or hand crank for controlled output

### 2. Add Battery Storage
Store excess power for use during low-output periods:
- **12V lead-acid battery**: $50–$100
- **Lithium battery**: $100–$300
- **Capacitor bank**: $20–$50

### 3. Use an Inverter for AC Power
Convert DC output to household AC:
- **Pure sine wave inverter**: $30–$150
- **Modified sine wave inverter**: $20–$80

### 4. Combine with Solar Panels
A hybrid approach maximizes total output:
- Magnetic generator provides night and cloudy-day power
- Solar panels provide daytime power
- Combined system reduces battery bank size

## Bottom Line

A Tesla magnetic generator can save you **$30–$144 per month** depending on size and prime mover. At the low end, you'll see payback in 3–7 months. At the high end with a professional build and strong wind or water drive, savings can exceed $100/month.

For most DIY builders, a mid-range build ($100–$300) offering $30–$72/month in savings represents the best balance of cost and return. Over 20 years, that's **$7,200–$17,280 in total savings** — far exceeding the initial investment.
