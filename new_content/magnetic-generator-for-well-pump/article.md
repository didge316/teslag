# Magnetic Generator for Well Pump: Deep Well Power (2026)

Deep well submersible pumps typically need 1,000–3,000W to start and 500–1,500W to run. A magnetic generator with battery storage can handle these surge loads reliably.

Here's how to power your well pump with a magnetic generator.

## Well Pump Sizing

| Well Depth | Pump Wattage | Generator Size | Battery Bank |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|-------------|
| Shallow (<50ft) | 500–800W | 500–1,000W | 100Ah @ 24V |
| Medium (50–150ft) | 800–1,500W | 1,000–2,000W | 200Ah @ 48V |
| Deep (150ft+) | 1,500–3,000W | 2,000–5,000W | 400Ah @ 48V |

## Best Setup

- **Generator:** Professional PMG or large DIY axial flux
- **Prime mover:** Wind turbine (best for well sites)
- **Battery:** Sized for 2–3 pump cycles minimum
- **Inverter:** 125% of pump running watts
- **Pressure tank:** 40–60 gallon minimum

## Conclusion

A magnetic generator can reliably power well pumps. Size for pump running watts (not starting watts) and use battery storage for surge handling.
