# Tesla Magnetic Generator for Shed Workshop: Combined Power Solution (2026)

A combined shed workshop generator powers both your storage shed and workshop space from a single system. This is one of the most practical applications for a DIY magnetic generator.

Here's how to design and install a combined shed workshop generator system.

## Best Combined Setup

- **Generator:** Large axial flux PMG or professional PMG (2–3kW)
- **Prime mover:** Wind turbine (50ft pole between buildings)
- **Battery:** 200–400Ah @ 48V LiFePO4
- **Inverter:** 3,000W pure sine wave
- **Panel:** Sub-panel in workshop with circuits to both buildings
- **Cost:** $3,000–$6,000

## Wiring Both Buildings

1. **Install sub-panel in workshop** — Main distribution point
2. **Run conduit to shed** — Underground, 4-wire (hot, hot, neutral, ground)
3. **Install breaker in shed** — Dedicated circuit for shed loads
4. **Use GFCI protection** — Required for both buildings

## Conclusion

A combined shed workshop generator is one of the most practical DIY magnetic generator applications. One system powers both buildings efficiently.
