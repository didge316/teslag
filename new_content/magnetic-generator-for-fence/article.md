# Magnetic Generator for Fence: Powered Electric Fencing (2026)

Electric fence energizers typically use mains power or batteries that need regular charging. A magnetic generator can keep your fence line charged continuously, 24/7, without grid power or battery replacements.

Here's how to use a magnetic generator for electric fencing.

## Fence Energizer Power Needs

| Energizer Type | Watts | Output Joules | Range |
|---------------|-------|--------------|-------|
| Small (pasture) | 50W | 0.5–1J | 10–20 miles of wire |
| Medium (multi-pasture) | 100W | 1–3J | 20–50 miles |
| Large (commercial) | 200W | 3–10J | 50–100+ miles |

Daily energy: 50–200W × 24h = 1.2–4.8 kWh, but energizers cycle on/off, so actual draw is 10–50W continuous.

## Best Setup for Fencing

### Solar-Assisted Magnetic Generator

- **Generator:** DIY bifilar pancake coil
- **Prime mover:** Small wind turbine (fence-line mounted)
- **Battery:** 50–100Ah LiFePO4
- **Energizer:** 12V electric fence energizer
- **Output:** 50–150W continuous

### Fence-Line Wind Turbine

Mount a small wind turbine along your fence line:
- **Height:** 10–15 feet (low-profile)
- **Output:** 50–200W
- **Coverage:** Powers energizer + charges battery
- **Cost:** $200–$600

## Fence-Specific Benefits

- **No grid connection needed** — Remote pastures, back acres
- **No battery replacement** — Lithium lasts 10+ years
- **Continuous charging** — Fence always energized
- **Weather resistant** — Outdoor-rated generator housing
- **Low visibility** — Small turbine blends with fence line

## Conclusion

A magnetic generator is ideal for remote electric fencing. A small wind-driven unit keeps your energizer charged year-round with zero fuel cost.
