# Magnetic Generator for Greenhouse: Year-Round Climate Control (2026)

A magnetic generator can power your greenhouse's heating, ventilation, lighting, and irrigation systems — keeping your plants thriving year-round without grid power or fuel costs.

Here's how to use a magnetic generator for greenhouse power.

## Best Generator Setup

### Summer (Low Demand)

- **Generator:** DIY bifilar pancake coil
- **Prime mover:** Small wind turbine
- **Battery:** 100Ah @ 24V LiFePO4
- **Inverter:** 500W pure sine wave
- **Output:** Covers all summer loads

### Winter (High Demand)

- **Generator:** Large axial flux PMG or professional PMG
- **Prime mover:** Wind turbine or hydro
- **Battery:** 200–400Ah @ 48V LiFePO4
- **Inverter:** 1,500–3,000W pure sine wave
- **Output:** Covers heaters, lights, fans

## Greenhouse-Specific Tips

1. **Insulate batteries** — Keep above freezing in winter
2. **Use thermal mass** — Water barrels store heat, reducing heater runtime
3. **Automate ventilation** — Reduces fan power needs
4. **LED grow lights** — 80% less power than HPS
5. **Wind turbine placement** — Mount above greenhouse roof for best exposure

## Conclusion

A magnetic generator can handle both summer and winter greenhouse loads. Size your system for winter peak demand and use battery storage to carry you through low-wind periods.
