## Homestead-Specific Tips

1. **Diversify prime movers** — Wind + hydro + solar = redundancy
2. **Size battery for 3 days autonomy** — Protects against low-wind periods
3. **Zoned electrical panels** — Separate circuits for each building
4. **Heat first** — Insulate before sizing generator for heating
5. **Monitor everything** — Battery monitor, inverter display, generator output
