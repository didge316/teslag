  • Phone charging: 15–30W
  • Sump pump: 300–600W (prevent flooding)
  • Well pump: 500–1,500W (water supply)
  • Total backup needs: 700–2,200W

Installation Tips for Emergency Backup

  1. Install a transfer switch — Switch between grid, generator, and battery seamlessly
  2. Place battery bank indoors — Protect from temperature extremes
  3. Add a charge controller — Prevents overcharging during long wind events
  4. Label all circuits — Know which outlets are on backup power
  5. Test monthly — Run your generator for 15 minutes to keep batteries charged
