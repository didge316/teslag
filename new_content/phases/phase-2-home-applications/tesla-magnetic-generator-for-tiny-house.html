With Heat Pump (Full Power)

  • Generator: Professional PMG (3–5kW)

Tiny House-Specific Design

Space Optimization

  • Wall-mounted generator — Saves floor space
  • Under-bench battery bank — Hidden storage
  • Rooftop wind turbine — Uses vertical space
  • Slim-profile inverter — Wall-mounted near panel

Insulation First

Before sizing your generator:

  • Insulate walls/roof — Reduces heating/cooling needs
  • Use heat pump — 3× more efficient than resistance heating
  • LED everywhere — Minimal lighting power
  • Energy Star appliances — Cuts fridge/washer power
