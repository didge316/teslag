# Magnetic Generator for Lights: LED Lighting Off-Grid (2026)

Lighting is the simplest and most affordable application for a magnetic generator. A small DIY unit can power LED lights for an entire home, cabin, or workshop for pennies per month.

Here's how to light your space with a magnetic generator.

## LED Power Needs

| Application | LED Watts | Hours/Day | Daily kWh |
|------------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 LED bulb | 10W | 6 | 0.06 |
| 5 LED bulbs | 50W | 6 | 0.30 |
| 10 LED bulbs | 100W | 6 | 0.60 |
| Entire home (20 bulbs) | 200W | 6 | 1.20 |

## Minimum Setup for Lighting

- **Generator:** DIY bifilar pancake coil (50–100W output)
- **Prime mover:** Bicycle drive or small wind turbine
- **Battery:** 50–100Ah LiFePO4 @ 12V or 24V
- **Inverter:** 300W pure sine wave (or 12V DC lighting)
- **Total cost:** $200–$500

## Direct DC Option

For lighting only, skip the inverter entirely:
- **12V or 24V LED strips/lights** — No inverter needed
- **Direct from battery** — 95%+ efficiency vs. 90% with inverter
- **Simpler wiring** — Fewer components
- **Cost savings:** $100–$300 on inverter

## Conclusion

Lighting is the easiest entry point for magnetic generator power. A $200–$500 system can light an entire home with LEDs.
