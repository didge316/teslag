## Minimum Setup for Phone Charging

### The Tiny Generator

- **Coil:** 12-turn bifilar pancake (2-inch form)
- **Magnets:** 4 neodymium magnets
- **Prime mover:** Hand crank or small fan
- **Battery:** 10,000mAh USB power bank
- **Output:** 5V USB directly or through small boost converter
- **Cost:** $20–$50

### The Practical Setup

- **Generator:** DIY bifilar pancake coil (100W)
- **Prime mover:** Bicycle drive or small wind turbine
- **Battery:** 200Ah LiFePO4 @ 48V
- **Inverter:** 500W pure sine wave
- **USB ports:** Multiple throughout home
- **Cost:** $400–$800

## USB-Only Setup

For phone/tablet charging only:
- **Generator → Charge controller → Battery → USB hub**
- No inverter needed
- 100% efficient DC-to-USB
- Simplest possible system
