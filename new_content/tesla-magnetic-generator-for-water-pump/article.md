# Tesla Magnetic Generator for Water Pump: Off-Grid Water Supply (2026)

A Tesla magnetic generator can power your well pump, irrigation pump, or water transfer system — providing reliable off-grid water access without fuel deliveries or grid power.

Here's how to size and wire a magnetic generator for water pumping.

## Water Pump Power Requirements

| Pump Type | Watts | Hours/Day | Daily kWh |
|-----------|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Small well pump (jet) | 500W | 2 | 1.00 |
| Submersible well pump | 1,000W | 1.5 | 1.50 |
| Irrigation pump | 1,500W | 3 | 4.50 |
| Water transfer pump | 300W | 4 | 1.20 |

## Best Generator Setup

### Well Pump Setup

- **Generator:** Axial flux PMG (1,000–2,000W)
- **Prime mover:** Wind turbine or hydro
- **Battery:** 200Ah @ 48V LiFePO4
- **Inverter:** 2,000W pure sine wave
- **Pressure tank:** Stores water, reduces pump runtime

### Hydro Option (Best)

If you have flowing water:
- **Generator:** Shaft-driven magnetic PMG
- **Prime mover:** Water wheel or turbine
- **Output:** 500–2,000W continuously
- **Best for:** Properties with streams or creeks

## Pump-Specific Tips

1. **Use a pressure tank** — Stores water, reduces pump cycling
2. **Start pump during high generation** — Charge batteries before pumping
3. **Solar assist** — Combine with solar panels for redundancy
4. **Gravity feed** — Store water at elevation, use gravity for distribution

## Conclusion

A magnetic generator is practical for water pumping, especially when combined with a pressure tank and battery storage. Hydro-driven setups are the most efficient.
