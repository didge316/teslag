## Best Setup for Garages

### Workshop-Only (No EV Charging)

- **Generator:** Large axial flux PMG (1,000–2,000W)
- **Prime mover:** Wind turbine or bicycle drive
- **Battery:** 200Ah @ 48V LiFePO4
- **Inverter:** 2,000–3,000W pure sine wave
- **Cost:** $2,000–$4,000

### With EV Charging

- **Generator:** Professional PMG (2–5kW)
- **Prime mover:** Wind turbine
- **Battery:** 400–600Ah @ 48V LiFePO4
- **Inverter:** 5,000W pure sine wave
- **Cost:** $5,000–$12,000
