Build a Magnetic Generator to Power Your Whole Home - Off Grid Electricity Guide

I bet you're pretty excited at the thought of getting off the grid! I know I was. Just the idea of how much money I could save made my head spin! I'm just so glad I started this project. It's been one amazing journey.

So did I end up powering my whole house? Keep reading!

The first thing you need to understand is, that the very first generator you build won't be the one to take you totally off the grid.

If you really want to build a magnetic generator to power your home, you will need to practice building some smaller prototypes first.

Most folks moan when their first magnetic generator only reduces their power bill by 40-50%. But if you ask me, that's still amazing. The thing is, the next one you build will always be better.

I'll cut straight to the chase. It took me 5 attempts before I made the tesla generator that took me totally off the grid. I ended up giving the first two to my neighbours. I think the third one is the one that I have powering my workshop. The other one isn't finished yet, but it's going to be a spare.

Start Small Before Building a Magnetic Generator to Power Your Home

Basically, don't go crazy with your first build. You should think of this as your test project and a good way to learn exactly what is needed to go fully off grid electricity. Remember, everyone has different power needs.

Once you've finished your first generator and hooked it up to your existing power supply you'll have a good idea of exactly what is involved in the whole construction process. After that, you will find it a lot easier to build a bigger version that can power your whole home.

It will also make it a lot easier to work out exactly how big you need to build your final version so that it's powerful enough to power your whole house. Most people start with a small Tesla magnet motor prototype before moving on to a full-size perpetual motion generator build.

I've had so much fun with this project. I'm still going to make another one because I think I can still improve it and make it more efficient. Plus, it's so much fun.
