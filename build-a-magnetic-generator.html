That really depends on you and your own skills. Learning how to build a magnetic generator doesn't take too long, but it depends on how much time you have to put into the project.

Anyway, learning how to build a magnetic generator is half the fun, isn't it? Take my advice and don't rush it. Go get your plans and enjoy the build!

How to Wire it Up to My Existing Power Supply?

Although this is covered within the plans, you need to be careful. In my opinion, this is a job for a qualified electrician.

Once you've built a couple of these free energy generators (believe me, you'll end up building more than one!), you'll definitely want to have one set up in your house permanently. This should be wired in by a professional.

So many house fires are caused by substandard wiring. Take my advice, spend a bit of cash and get a qualified electrician. It's just safer and you'll be saving a fortune on electricity bills by that point anyway!
