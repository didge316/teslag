**2. What I actually know, and where I'm guessing.** CSS Overflow 3 defines the scrollable overflow region as the union of border boxes of descendants *for which the scroll container is the containing block*, and explicitly excludes boxes positioned relative to an ancestor outside the scroll container. A `position:absolute` element with no positioned ancestor has the initial containing block — i.e. the viewport — as its containing block, so per spec its 315+150 right edge **is** in the document's scrollable overflow. On that text WebKit looks correct and Chromium looks like the deviation, not the reverse. I do **not** know a WebKit bug number for this and I am not going to invent one; I also cannot confirm the usual folklore that WebKit includes `position:fixed` overflow where Blink does not. Treat my engine-specific claims as guesses and the spec claim as solid.
