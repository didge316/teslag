# Sticky bar — what the module review produced

Owner-facing. Everything below cites evidence that exists on the branch or in `tests/`; anything I could not verify first-hand is marked UNVERIFIED rather than softened. Nine items, none of which is a push blocker unless the last line says so. They cost roughly ten lines of code between them plus three decisions only the owner can make.

**Component footprint.** 22 rule blocks over 17 distinct selectors: 16 `tmg-cta-*` plus `.tmg-sr-only` (`css/styles-custom.css:431`). An earlier count of "21 blocks, 16 selectors" in the commit message was produced by a `tmg-cta`-scoped inventory describing a `tmg-` footprint, and could not see `.tmg-sr-only`, which `buildBar()` depends on for the CTA's text alternative. Recount and the commit message are corrected; `dormant-bar-css.py` now strips `tmg-` and asserts 22.

## 1. Dismissal is browser-wide for 30 minutes, not per-tab
- Where: `js/scripts.js` `rememberDismissal()` (line UNVERIFIED), CONFIG `DISMISS_COOKIE_TTL`.
- Evidence: dismissed on `/whole-house.html`; a second tab in the same browser context showed `barShown False`, its own `sessionStorage` flag `None`, cookie present. Only the cookie suppresses it.
- Fix: none required — this is a decision, not a defect.
- Cost: none to leave; ~3 lines to change.
- Blocker: no.
- Open question, deliberately not answered here: browser-wide 30 minutes (fewer repeat impressions, no nag) versus per-tab semantics (what CONFIG claims as the reason `sessionStorage` is primary). To get per-tab, write the cookie only inside the `setItem` catch. The comment currently claims the second and the code does the first.

## 2. Comment contradicts the code: "remembered permanently"
- Where: `js/scripts.js:158`.
- Evidence: 322 lines above an 1800-second cookie.
- Fix: replace with "remembered for the visit, or half an hour where storage is unavailable".
- Cost: one line. Blocker: no.

## 3. `wp-textsize` attribution is fiction, and it is pre-existing
- Where: `js/scripts.js:184`, `css/styles-custom.css:312`, `css/styles-custom.css:528`, plus `tests/overflow/payload-measured-322b32b3533a.css:276`.
- Evidence: zero HTML files on the branch mention `wp-textsize`; all four occurrences are comments. The string appears in the pre-fix payload snapshot, so item 7 shipped a stylesheet already claiming it and the module inherits the claim. The behaviour the design guards against is real — root font grows under zoom and user font settings, which is what the 1,872-cell matrix drove — only the attribution is wrong.
- Fix: rewrite the three comments to attribute the behaviour to reader zoom and user font settings, and strike the control. A comment-only CSS edit is provably rule-text-neutral: `tests/overflow/css-comment-only.py` exists for exactly that check and must pass before the edit lands.
- Cost: three comments, one check run. Blocker: no.

## 4. Dismissal cookie has no `Secure`
- Where: `js/scripts.js` `rememberDismissal()`, cookie write (line UNVERIFIED).
- Evidence: `KEY + '=1; path=/; SameSite=Lax; Max-Age=' + TTL`; the site is HTTPS-only.
- Fix: append `; Secure`.
- Cost: five characters. Blocker: no.

## 5. Dismiss removes three listeners and leaves a fourth
- Where: `js/scripts.js:440-442` removes `scroll`, `resize`, bar `focusout`; `js/scripts.js:457` adds `resize → growPad`, which survives.
- Evidence: `growPad`'s first line returns unless `is-visible`, so it is inert after dismissal.
- Fix: remove it in the same handler, or comment why it stays.
- Cost: one line. Blocker: no.

## 6. The bar is tabbable behind an `aria-modal="true"` dialog
- Where: `buildBar()` appends to `<body>`; `js/exit-popup.js` declares `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"` and never makes the page inert.
- Evidence: `/build-a-perpetual-motion-generator`, 390x844, chromium and webkit, popup raised by the real 25s dwell and by a synthetic exit-intent `mouseout`: bar visible behind it, its two controls — one of them the paid link — in the tab ring. 21 focusables sit outside that dialog, 19 of them the page's own, so the popup predates this and has never had a trap.
- Fix, in order: (a) one condition in `paint()` to hold the bar away while `[role="dialog"]` is open, ~3 lines, ships with or reverts with the bar; (b) `inert` on the page wrapper while the dialog is up — one attribute, universally supported, fixes all 21 and makes `aria-modal` stop being a lie. Do not hand-roll a focus trap; `inert` is less code and strictly more correct.
- Cost: ~10 lines across two commits. Blocker: no — the bar cannot have made the popup worse, since before this commit it did not exist.

## 7. Attribution: split `/can-I-buy` out of the fallback bucket
- Where: `TID_RULES` in `js/scripts.js` (lines UNVERIFIED).
- Evidence: 50 of 52 pages render the bar; `sticky_article` holds 36 of them — 72% — including `/can-I-buy`, `/build-a-*`, `/whole-house`, `/magnetic-generator`. Offline and live tid reads agree on every rendering page, so this is not a matcher bug, it is an under-specified rule set.
- Fix: minimum viable is one anchored rule for `/can-I-buy`, because bottom-of-funnel EPC is the whole thesis. Either split the intent pages properly or collapse to two tids and stop presenting a breakdown.
- Cost: one regex. Blocker: no, but it decides whether the bar's numbers can ever say anything.

## 8. `/magnetic-generator-scam` still shows the offer
- Where: `buildBar()`/`init()`, no suppression path; attributed `sticky_scam`, measured.
- Evidence: the page that argues the product is a scam gets a $47 offer 700px down it.
- Fix: suppress the bar on that path. One condition.
- Cost: two lines. Blocker: no — owner's call, and I would make it.

## 9. Price and guarantee are assertions no test can falsify
- Where: `js/scripts.js:299-300`, both string constants.
- Evidence: `~$47 · instant download · 60-day money-back guarantee`, and the sub-390px short variant. Both are claims about a third-party product, shown to an audience that includes `/magnetic-generator-scam`.
- Fix: owner gives a dated yes, and a date for the next check.
- Cost: a decision. Blocker: no, but it is the only item here that can be true today and false next quarter with nothing on the site changing.

## §12 — Review rounds 9-11: two statistics that were not facts, and one measurement that only the code could settle

**The reviewer could not read the branch, and that is a property of the branches rather than of the reviewer.** This work exists only on `fix/alt-nav-overflow` and `feature/sticky-offer-bar` on the desktop box, unpushed by the owner's rule; the reviewer's checkout is a July `main` on a different lineage with no object from either branch. Everything they reviewed from round 9 onward was pasted text, and the review state in the bar commit says so rather than pretending the branch was read. The consequence is worth recording as a method: a review of pasted text can certify a blob only if the paste is identified — which is why the commit message carries `da605e24…` and why "reviewed" there means "reviewed at that hash", not "reviewed the branch".

**Two of the findings in this stretch turned out to be defects in the statistics rather than in the tree.** The first: a "21 rule blocks over 16 selectors" footprint, generated by a brace-matched inventory filtered on the substring `tmg-cta`, describing a component whose real namespace is `tmg-` — it could not see `.tmg-sr-only`, which `buildBar()` depends on for the CTA's text alternative, and the resulting alarm ("the sr-only class is missing, the CTA will render the new-tab notice as visible text") was produced entirely by the instrument. Recounted: **22 blocks, 17 selectors**. The second, one round earlier: "3 rules" for the bar's CSS, from `grep -c 'tmg-cta-bar'`, a line count that described none of the component and could have meant the tier rules were missing. Both were caught by asking whether a number could be reconciled against the component's known shape — the discipline that found the WebKit instrument bug in §11, applied to arithmetic instead of browsers.

**Dismissal is browser-wide for thirty minutes, and no comment in the repository said so.** Measured rather than read: dismiss on `/whole-house.html`, open a second tab in the same browser context — fresh `sessionStorage`, shared cookies, which is what a second tab is — and the bar is gone, its own session flag `None`, the cookie present. `rememberDismissal()` writes both stores unconditionally, so the fallback is never a fallback and the per-tab semantics that CONFIG gives as the reason `sessionStorage` is primary never occur. The choice between browser-wide-half-an-hour and true per-tab is a product question and is left open in `bar-module-followups.md`; what is not open is that the code and the comment currently describe different products.

**`wp-textsize` appears in zero HTML files, and the string has been justifying a design since before this branch.** Four occurrences exist in the whole tree, all of them comments: `css/styles-custom.css:312`, `:528`, `js/scripts.js:184`, and the pre-fix payload snapshot at `tests/overflow/payload-measured-322b32b3533a.css:276`. The string is a WordPress-template artefact. The behaviour the deterministic-height design guards against is entirely real — root font grows under reader zoom and user font settings, which is what the 1,872-cell matrix drove — but the attribution is fiction, and it is pre-existing: item 7 shipped a stylesheet already claiming a control that does not exist. Correcting three comments is provably rule-text-neutral, and `tests/overflow/css-comment-only.py` is the check that exists for exactly that claim.

**The modal finding is measured, in both engines, through both triggers.** `js/exit-popup.js` declares `aria-modal="true"` and makes nothing inert; the bar appends to `<body>` and stays tabbable behind the dialog, with the paid link among the controls reachable. On `/build-a-perpetual-motion-generator` at 390x844 in chromium and webkit, with the popup raised by the real 25-second dwell and by a synthetic exit-intent `mouseout`, the bar remained visible and both its controls sat in the tab ring. The scoping number that decides the fix: **21 focusables outside the dialog, 19 of them the page's own** — this is a pre-existing defect that the bar joins, with one paid link. Hence the recommendation is a three-line `paint()` guard for the bar plus `inert` on the page wrapper for the rest, and the honest statement that the guard belongs with the bar's revert and `inert` belongs to the owner.

**What is owner-blocked, and why it is the owner's.** Three decisions cannot be made from this branch: whether dismissal is browser-wide for half an hour or per-tab; whether the $47 and the 60-day guarantee still stand, which no test can see and which the site's own `/magnetic-generator-scam` page makes expensive to get wrong; and whether the offer should appear on that page at all. Two smaller ones are cheap enough to bundle with a deployment — splitting `/can-I-buy` out of the 72% fallback bucket so the EPC table can say something, and the `Secure` flag. None of these blocks the push; all of them are the difference between a bar that can be judged and a bar that cannot.
