How Much Noise Do Magnetic Generators Make?

One thing to consider when building your own magnetic generator is just how much noise it will make. Many people who are thinking of making their own generator are concerned about this.

Will it keep the kids awake? Will it disturb the neighbours? These are all real concerns. So in this article, we’ll find out just how much noise does a free energy generator make.

The first thing to understand is that there isn’t just one straightforward answer to this question. The reason for this is that there are different variations and different sizes of the Tesla magnetic generator that you can build.

A Bigger Tesla Generator Means More Noise

If you are thinking of building a magnetic generator to power your whole house, the noise output will be significantly more than a smaller-scale generator.

The good news is though, whatever the size, the noise output is actually pretty low. The sound produced by a Tesla magnetic generator is often far less than that of a regular solar power inverter.

The size of the generator is the main thing that determines how loud it is. There are, however, some things that can reduce the noise output.

This is one of the best parts about building a Tesla generator. You get to customize it and make it your own design. The Tesla magnetic generator plans show you how to build a standard generator but there are many things you can do to enhance the design to your own needs.

How to Run Your Magnetic Generator Silently

I’ve built several generators over the last few years. Here are just a few things that I’ve done to make my magnetic generators virtually silent.

Use noise-dampening foam on the support mounts. I’ve found this reduces any vibrations and reduces noise.

Build a soundproof housing. This can be as simple as a plywood box lined with noise-dampening foam. I actually did a budget build once and used egg boxes on the inside of the plywood. It turned out pretty good!

Spend more time balancing the wheel. I found this to be really important. Take my advice and spend more time here because if it’s aligned properly, it greatly reduces the sound output.

Get some decent grease on the axels. This not only reduces noise but can also increase the power output of your magnetic generator.
