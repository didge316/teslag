Perpetual Motion Generator vs Magnetic Generator?

If you're wondering whether a perpetual motion generator and a magnetic generator are different devices, the answer is no, they are in fact the same thing. A magnetic generator (also called a magnetic energy generator, free energy generator, or Tesla generator) uses permanent magnets arranged in a specific pattern to create continuous perpetual motion.

This endless spinning motion is then used to generate electricity, producing more power than it consumes. The reality is, that's exactly what a perpetual motion generator refers to!

Tesla invented a machine that keeps moving indefinitely (or appears to) without needing external energy input after it's started.

The confusion often comes from online debates about whether true perpetual motion is possible (regular physics often says no due to friction and losses), but thousands of hobbyists have built working magnetic generators that significantly reduce electricity bills using the same principles.

Whether you call it a perpetual motion generator or a magnetic generator, you're talking about the same DIY device.
